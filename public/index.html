Frame & Voice

Pairs what was on screen with what was said, so an AI sees the recording instead of only reading it. Videos stay on your device, including when it transcribes. When you ask a question, only the transcript text and your question go to the AI.

To install on iPhone or iPad: tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.

3Ask about the recording

The AI reads the transcript and your on-screen notes, not the pictures. Only that text and your question are sent to it; the video stays on your device.

Other ways to ask

Copies the recording's details with your question, then opens the site. Paste into the message box with Ctrl+V (Cmd+V on Mac) and send. ChatGPT works without an account.

Take it elsewhere

The context pack is the recording as text: what was on screen, your notes and what was said, moment by moment. The storyboard PDF adds the pictures, for AIs that accept files.

Getting the best answer

AIs that only get text can't see the frames, so a short line in "On screen" for the frames that matter helps most, for example "Revenue table, total row highlighted". To show one specific screen, use Copy image on that frame and paste it straight into the chat.

Frame & Voice works offline once loaded, apart from asking questions. Videos and frames never leave your device; the transcript text is sent only when you ask a question, to an AI model hosted by Groq. Transcription uses OpenAI's open-source Whisper model, downloaded once and run on your device.
